# 🛍️ E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard (Microsoft Excel)

---

# 📌 Project Overview

The **E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard developed to analyze sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product popularity, order channels, and delivery efficiency.

The dashboard enables business users to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify sales trends, compare customer segments, and make informed business decisions through dynamic visualizations and slicers.

---

# 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Track total orders, revenue, and quantity sold.
- Identify top-performing product categories.
- Understand customer purchasing patterns.
- Compare sales across different order channels.
- Evaluate shipping performance.
- Analyze customer demographics.
- Visualize regional sales distribution.

## 📊 Dashboard Components

## 📅 Weekly Sales Trend

Tracks weekly performance for:

- Quantity Sold
- Revenue Generated

### Insights

- Revenue remains consistent throughout the weeks.
- Peak sales are observed around **Week 10**.
- Quantity sold closely follows revenue trends.

---

## 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Customer Distribution by Gender & Order Mode

Analyzes purchases by:

- Female
- Male
- Other
- Unknown

Across different sales channels:

- App
- Instagram
- Partner App
- Target.com
- Website

### Insights

- Female customers contribute the highest percentage of sales.
- Website and App generate the largest customer traffic.
- Other gender categories contribute only a small percentage.
